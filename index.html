About John's Academy

Preparing Today's Learners for Tomorrow's World

At our school, we believe education should go beyond textbooks and examinations. Our vision is to nurture future-ready individuals who are confident, curious, adaptable and equipped to navigate an ever-changing world.

Through a balanced blend of academics, experiential learning, practical exploration and creative expression, we encourage students to think critically, communicate effectively and approach challenges with confidence.

We place equal importance on sports, fitness and extracurricular activities — recognising that physical well-being, discipline, teamwork and resilience are essential to a child's overall development.

Parents Ask

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about classes, medium of instruction, age criteria, transport and admissions at John's Academy Medchal.

What classes does John's Academy offer?

John's Academy offers education from Nursery to Grade 10: Nursery, LKG and UKG, Primary (Grades 1–5), Middle School (Grades 6–8) and Secondary (Grades 9–10).

Which board does John's Academy follow?

John's Academy follows the SSC (Secondary School Certificate) curriculum of the Telangana State Board of Secondary Education.

What is the medium of instruction at John's Academy?

John's Academy is an English medium school. All classes from Nursery to Grade 10 are taught in English, and Telugu is taught as a language subject as per the Telangana State Board syllabus.

Where is John's Academy located?

John's Academy has two campuses open today: the Medchal Campus in Medchal, Hyderabad (PIN 501401), and the Ramanthapur – Chegunta Campus near Chegunta in Medak district, north of Hyderabad. A third campus in Toopran is coming soon.

Which areas near Medchal does John's Academy serve?

The Medchal Campus is convenient for families in Medchal and nearby areas including Kompally, Dundigal, Suraram, Bowrampet, Keesara, Petbasheerabad and Alwal.

Does John's Academy Medchal provide school transport?

John's Academy offers school transport for students in Medchal and nearby areas including Kompally, Dundigal and Suraram. Call our admissions team on +91 90308 69502 to check the routes near your home.

What is the age criteria for Nursery, LKG and UKG at John's Academy?

Nursery is for children aged 2.5 to 3.5 years, LKG for 3.5 to 4.5 years, and UKG for 4.5 to 5.5 years. Please call +91 90308 69502 to confirm the exact cut-off date for the upcoming academic year.

Are admissions open at John's Academy for 2027–28?

Yes. Admissions for the 2027–28 academic year are open for all classes from Nursery to Grade 10. Seats fill quickly, especially in Nursery, LKG and UKG, so we recommend enquiring early.

How do I apply for admission to John's Academy?

There are three steps: submit an enquiry on this website or call +91 90308 69502; visit the campus and meet our teachers; then complete the admission formalities. Our admissions team contacts every enquiry within 24 hours.

What is the fee structure at John's Academy?

Fees depend on the class. Call our admissions team on +91 90308 69502 or submit an enquiry, and we will share the full fee details for your child's class during your campus visit.

How can I contact John's Academy?

Call or WhatsApp +91 90308 69502, email johnsacademy.mdcl@johnfoundation.org, or fill in the admission enquiry form on this website.
